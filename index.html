SCUDERIA FERRARI

RACING IN THE BLOOD

THE PRANCING HORCE

Scuderia Ferrari, also known as “The Prancing Horse,” is the oldest and most successful team in the history of Formula One. Founded in 1929 by Enzo Ferrari, the team originally managed racing cars for Alfa Romeo before Ferrari began producing its own cars in 1947. Based in Maranello, Ferrari has competed in every Formula One season since the championship began in 1950 and has achieved more victories, podiums, pole positions, and championships than any other team in the sport. Legendary drivers such as Michael Schumacher, Niki Lauda, Kimi Räikkönen, and today’s stars Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton have raced for the iconic Italian team. Ferrari is famous not only for its racing success but also for its passionate fanbase, the “Tifosi,” who treat every race weekend like a national festival.

THE CONTRUCTORS CHAMPIONS

Scuderia Ferrari has won the Formula One Constructors’ Championship 16 times, making it the most successful constructor in F1 history. The Constructors’ Championship is awarded to the team that scores the highest total points across a season using both of its drivers’ results. Ferrari won its first constructors’ title in 1961 and became especially dominant during the early 2000s when legends like Michael Schumacher, Rubens Barrichello, and technical mastermind Ross Brawn helped the team secure six consecutive constructors’ championships from 1999 to 2004. Ferrari’s success has come from a combination of powerful engineering, strategic racing, and iconic cars developed in Maranello, Italy. Even during difficult seasons, Ferrari remains one of the most respected and followed constructors in motorsport because of its unmatched history, passionate Tifosi fanbase, and deep connection to Formula One itself.

THE DRIVERS CHAMPIONS

Scuderia Ferrari has been home to some of the greatest drivers in Formula One history. Legendary champions such as Michael Schumacher, Niki Lauda, Alberto Ascari, Kimi Räikkönen, and Sebastian Vettel have all raced in Ferrari’s iconic red cars. Schumacher remains Ferrari’s most successful driver, winning five consecutive Drivers’ Championships with the team from 2000 to 2004 and helping create one of the most dominant eras in motorsport history. Ferrari drivers are known not only for speed and skill but also for handling immense pressure, as racing for Ferrari carries the expectations of millions of passionate Tifosi fans around the world. Today, drivers like Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton continue Ferrari’s legacy, aiming to bring the championship back to Maranello.

THE HISTORY

Scuderia Ferrari was founded in 1929 by Enzo Ferrari in Modena, Italy, beginning as a small racing organisation that managed and prepared Alfa Romeo race cars. After World War II, Ferrari started building its own cars in 1947, laying the foundation for what would become the most legendary name in motorsport history. In 1950, Ferrari entered the inaugural Formula One World Championship and has remained the only team to compete in every single season of the sport ever since. Over the decades, Ferrari transformed Formula One through innovation, engineering brilliance, and unforgettable rivalries, creating an identity that extends far beyond racing itself. Drivers such as Alberto Ascari, Niki Lauda, Michael Schumacher, and today Charles Leclerc have carried the pressure and pride of wearing Ferrari red, each contributing to the team’s historic journey. Ferrari’s golden eras, especially during the 1970s and the dominant Schumacher years from 2000 to 2004, established the team as the benchmark of excellence in Formula One. Yet Ferrari’s importance is not measured only by championships or records. The team represents passion, tradition, and emotion in a sport driven by speed and precision. The iconic prancing horse logo has become one of the most recognised symbols in global sports, representing ambition, prestige, and the relentless pursuit of perfection. Ferrari’s passionate supporters, famously known as the Tifosi, create an atmosphere unmatched in motorsport, turning race weekends into emotional celebrations of loyalty and pride. Through triumphs, setbacks, and decades of evolution, Ferrari has remained the heart of Formula One — a team whose legacy inspires millions of fans, drivers, and engineers to dream bigger, push harder, and chase greatness at every lap..

TEAM PRINCIPAL

THE PRIDE OF MARENELLO-FERRARI FACTORY

Ferrari Factory is the main manufacturing and engineering center of Ferrari, located in Maranello. Established by Enzo Ferrari, the factory is where Ferrari designs, develops, and builds both its Formula One cars and world-famous road cars. Known for combining advanced technology with traditional Italian craftsmanship, the facility includes production lines, research and development departments, wind tunnels, and racing operations. Close to the factory is the legendary Fiorano Circuit, Ferrari’s private test track where race and road cars are tested and refined. The Maranello factory is considered one of the most iconic automotive facilities in the world because it represents Ferrari’s commitment to performance, innovation, precision engineering, and racing excellence.

THE FUTURE

The future of Scuderia Ferrari represents a new era built on innovation, young talent, and the pursuit of returning to championship dominance. As Formula One moves toward more advanced hybrid technology, sustainable fuels, and stricter engineering regulations, Ferrari continues investing heavily in research, aerodynamics, and performance development to remain at the front of the grid. With drivers like Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton leading the team into a modern generation of racing, Ferrari aims to combine experience, speed, and strategic improvement to challenge for future world titles. Beyond Formula One, Ferrari is also shaping the future of automotive engineering through hybrid supercars, cutting-edge design, and sustainable innovation while maintaining the passion and identity that define the prancing horse. No matter how Formula One evolves, Ferrari’s goal remains unchanged — to lead, inspire, and continue writing new chapters in motorsport history.
